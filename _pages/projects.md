### CoRe 5G

**[Centers of Vocational Excellence for Resilient 5G Networks](https://core5g.eu/)**<br>
Work Package Leader · European Commission, Erasmus+ · 2026–2030

<a href="https://core5g.eu/"><img src="{{ '/images/projects/core5g-logo.png' | relative_url }}" alt="CoRe 5G project logo" width="360" height="98" style="max-width: 100%; height: auto;" loading="lazy"></a>

CoRe 5G develops vocational education and training for resilient, secure, and sustainable 5G networks. It connects education providers, industry, and research organizations through regional Centres of Vocational Excellence, practical training environments, and international collaboration.

### BeNiFIt

**[Fully Biocompatible Intrabody Nanoscale Communication System to Foster Novel In-Body Diagnostics and Monitoring Systems](https://ethungshanshitiri.github.io/benifit/)**<br>
Principal Investigator · European Commission, Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions · 2024–2026

<a href="https://ethungshanshitiri.github.io/benifit/"><img src="{{ '/images/projects/benifit-graphical-abstract.png' | relative_url }}" alt="BeNiFIt graphical abstract showing diagnostic challenges, intrabody nanonetworks, expected impact, and work packages" width="680" height="480" style="max-width: 100%; height: auto;" loading="lazy"></a>

BeNiFIt investigated intrabody nanoscale communication for biomarker detection and disease monitoring. The fellowship focused on modelling, simulation, and communication design to study how nanoscale sensing systems could support earlier and less invasive diagnostics.

### Targeted Drug Delivery using Cooperative Molecular Communications

Scientific Lead · National Research Foundation of Korea · 2021–2024

Studied how communication and coordination among multiple nanomachines can support targeted drug delivery.

### Synchronization and MAC in Molecular Communication Networks

Scientific Lead · National Research Foundation of Korea · 2017–2020

Developed synchronization and medium access control methods for coordinating transmissions in molecular communication networks.
